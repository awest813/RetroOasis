Both Game Boys run here, joined by an emulated link cable. You play Console 1; your guest plays Console 2 from their own device on this Wi-Fi, using their own cartridge and save. Use the game’s trade or link menu as usual.

Use your game’s battery save (.sav or .srm) to trade from your existing game. Without one, Console 1 starts a new game. Export saves from any emulator or from RetroOasis Saves.

Keyboard: arrows move · Z = B · X = A · Enter = Start · Shift = Select · Q/W = L/R (GBA). A gamepad also works.

Save in-game after every trade, then download saves before ending. Saves are not written back to your library automatically.
