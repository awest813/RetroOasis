Join a room

Play a game your friend is hosting, right here in your browser.

It’s in your friend’s invite. You can paste the whole invite link too.

  1. Same Wi-Fi. Be on the same Wi-Fi as your friend. (Far apart? You can both join a free app like Tailscale instead.) Guest Wi-Fi often blocks this.
  2. Open the invite. Scan your friend’s QR code or open their link. The room code fills in for you.
  3. Play. You get your own player number and see their game. Use a keyboard, the touch screen or a game controller.

Online rooms: you play on your friend’s screen; you don’t need the game. Trade & link: bring your own Game Boy or GBA game (and save) to trade or battle. Want to host? See Online play in Settings.

      Your game runs on your friend’s computer, connected to theirs. Add your save file (.sav or .srm) to play with your own progress; without it you start a new game.

      Save in the game after trading, then choose Download my save. Your friend’s computer doesn’t keep it.
