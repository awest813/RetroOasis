RETRO OASISLAN multiplayer

Join a local room

Connect to the host’s Wi-Fi or LAN, then open their invite. The host runs the game; you only need this browser.

Use the 10-character code from the host’s invite, or paste the whole invite link.

To host: open a real NES, SNES, Mega Drive or N64 game in RetroOasis and choose Host multiplayer. N64 supports two to four players and is experimental. For Game Boy, Game Boy Color and GBA trading or link play, choose Trade & link on the game instead.

Your cartridge

Your game runs as Console 2 on the host’s computer, linked to theirs. Add your battery save (.sav / .srm) to trade from your own game; without one you start a new game.

Save in-game after trading, then choose Save to this device. Your file is not kept on the host.
