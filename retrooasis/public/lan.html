Join a room

Play a friend’s online room or Trade & link from this browser.

The 10-character code from the host’s invite. Pasting the whole invite link works too.

  1. Same network. Join the host’s Wi-Fi, or the same virtual LAN (Tailscale, Nebula, ZeroTier) to play over the internet. Guest Wi-Fi often blocks this.
  2. Open the invite. Scan the host’s QR code or open their link; the room code fills in.
  3. Play. You get your own controller seat and see the host’s screen. Keyboard, touch and gamepads work.

Online rooms: you play on the host’s screen; no game needed. Trade & link: bring your own Game Boy or GBA game and save. Want to host? Open a game in RetroOasis on a computer running a room host. Online play settings
